FAQ

Straight answers.

How much can I actually earn?

30% of each referred client's BRIX subscription, every month. For example, 10 clients on the $29 plan earn you $87/month. If they run both Convert and Try On, that's $174/month, or $2,088 a year, recurring. Use the calculator for your own numbers.

How long do I get paid?

For life. As long as your referred client stays on a paid BRIX plan, you keep earning 30%.

Does it cost me or my clients anything extra?

No. There's no joining fee, card or course for you. Your clients pay the normal BRIX price, never more because of you.

How are referrals tracked and paid?

We set up your partner account on the call and walk you through exactly how referrals are tracked and how payouts work, before you refer anyone.

Will this create work for my team?

No. Both apps install in one click from the Shopify App Store, and BRIX supports your clients on the apps. Your job is the recommendation.

My clients aren't fashion brands. Is it still worth it?

Yes. Brix Convert works on any Shopify store. Try On is the extra for fashion and accessories clients.

What happens on the call?

30 minutes with our team. We look at your client mix, show which app suits which store, explain the partner terms and set you up. If it isn't a fit, you've lost nothing.
